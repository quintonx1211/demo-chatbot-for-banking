# Loans and Mortgages

**doc_id:** KB-LOAN-001
**owner:** Lending Operations
**last_reviewed:** 2026-04-30

## Application processing times

Personal loan applications are decided within 2 business days of a complete
submission. Auto loans take 2–3 business days. Residential mortgage applications
take 15–21 business days, and the clock starts only once the property appraisal
has been received. An application missing documents is placed in "Pending
Documents" and does not progress until the customer uploads what is outstanding.

## Application statuses

Applications move through five statuses: Submitted, Pending Documents, In Review,
Approved, and Declined. "In Review" means an underwriter has the file. A declined
application can be re-submitted after 90 days, or immediately if the customer has
new supporting income documentation.

## Required documents

Personal loans require photo identification, two recent pay stubs, and the most
recent tax return. Mortgages additionally require two years of tax returns, a
property purchase agreement, and proof of homeowner's insurance. Self-employed
applicants must provide two years of business tax returns and a year-to-date
profit and loss statement.

## Interest rates

Rates are set weekly and depend on credit profile, term, and loan-to-value ratio.
As of the last review, personal loans ranged from 8.99% to 17.99% APR, auto loans
from 6.49% to 12.99% APR, and 30-year fixed mortgages from 6.25% to 7.10% APR.
Published ranges are indicative; the rate on a specific application is confirmed
only in the written offer.

## Early repayment

Personal and auto loans can be repaid early at any time with no penalty.
Mortgages originated before 2023 may carry a prepayment penalty of up to 2% of the
outstanding balance within the first three years - the loan agreement is
authoritative for any individual account.
