# Funds Availability Policy

**doc_id:** KB-FUNDS-001
**owner:** Deposit Operations
**last_reviewed:** 2026-07-01
**supersedes:** KB-FUNDS-001 rev. 2025-06-30

This policy states when funds deposited to a transaction account become
available for withdrawal. It is written to the federal availability schedule and
is applied uniformly across branch, ATM and mobile channels except where a
channel-specific cut-off is stated below.

## Business days and cut-off times

A business day is a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a federal holiday. A
deposit received after the cut-off time for its channel, or on a day that is not
a business day, is treated as received on the next business day.

| Channel | Cut-off | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Branch teller | 4:00 pm local | Lobby hours may end earlier; the cut-off governs |
| Branch night depository | 8:00 am next business day | Bag opened under dual control |
| ATM (deposit-taking) | 9:00 pm | Applies to the ATM's local time, not the account's |
| Mobile deposit | 7:00 pm ET | Single national cut-off, not local time |
| Wire received | 5:00 pm ET | Fedwire operating day governs |

Mobile deposit is the only channel whose cut-off is stated in Eastern Time. A
customer in Pacific Time therefore loses an hour relative to the ATM schedule,
which is a frequent source of confusion and should be stated plainly when asked.

## Standard availability schedule

| Deposit type | Available |
|---|---|
| Cash deposited at a teller | Same business day |
| Cash deposited at an ATM | Next business day |
| Electronic direct deposit and incoming wire | Same business day received |
| Cheques (first $275) | Next business day |
| Cheques (remainder) | Second business day |
| Cheques drawn on this bank | Next business day in full |
| Money orders and cashier's cheques | Next business day in full |

The first $275 of the total of all cheques deposited on one banking day is made
available on the next business day. This is a per-day aggregate, not a per-item
amount: three $200 cheques deposited together release $275 next day and the
balance on the second business day, not $200 each.

## Exception holds

Availability may be extended beyond the standard schedule in the circumstances
listed below. An exception hold is not automatic; it is applied case by case and
requires the notice described further down.

| Exception | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Large deposit | Cheques totalling more than $6,725 on one banking day |
| New account | Account open less than 30 calendar days |
| Redeposited cheque | Item previously returned unpaid |
| Repeated overdraft | Account overdrawn on 6 or more banking days in the last 6 months |
| Reasonable doubt | Information leading the bank to doubt collectability |
| Emergency conditions | Communications or equipment failure, or a similar event |

Where an exception applies, funds are generally available no later than the
seventh business day after the day of deposit.

Two limits on the large-deposit exception are frequently misread. First, it
applies only to the amount **in excess of** $6,725; the first $6,725 follows the
standard schedule for that item type. Second, it applies only to cheques. Cash
and electronic payments are never subject to the large-deposit exception, no
matter the amount - a $50,000 incoming wire is available the same business day
it is received.

The repeated-overdraft exception is measured over the six months preceding the
deposit, and counts banking days on which the account was overdrawn at any point
during the day, not the number of overdraft items.

## New accounts

During the first 30 calendar days after an account is opened, the standard
schedule does not apply to cheque deposits. Electronic direct deposits and
incoming wires are available on the day received throughout this period. The
first $6,725 of any one day's deposit of cashier's cheques, certified cheques
and government cheques payable to the customer is available on the next business
day; the remainder is available on the ninth business day.

The 30-day period runs from the day the account is opened, not from the day of
the first deposit and not from the day the debit card is activated.

## Notice when a hold is placed

If a hold is placed at the time of deposit, the customer is given written notice
at that time. If the decision is made after the deposit is accepted - which
happens when a deposit is reviewed after the fact - notice is sent by the first
business day after the decision, to the address of record or by secure message
where the customer has elected electronic delivery.

The notice states the amount held, the reason drawn from the table above, and
the date the funds will be available. A hold placed without that notice is
released and the funds made available under the standard schedule.

## Longer holds on cheques deposited by mobile

Cheques deposited through the mobile app follow the standard schedule with one
difference: the second-business-day release applies to the remainder only after
the item has been successfully presented. Where a mobile-deposited item is
returned for image quality, the deposit is reversed and the customer is notified
by push notification and secure message, and the item must be redeposited in
branch.

## What this policy does not cover

Availability is not the same as finality. A cheque that has been made available
under this schedule may still be returned unpaid weeks later, and the account
will be debited when that happens. The customer remains responsible for the
amount of any item returned unpaid, including where the funds were withdrawn in
reliance on this schedule.
