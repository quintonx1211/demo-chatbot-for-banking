# Complaints, Errors and Regulatory Rights

**doc_id:** KB-REG-001
**owner:** Compliance and Customer Advocacy
**last_reviewed:** 2026-06-30

## Raising a complaint

A complaint can be raised in branch, by phone, through secure message in online
banking, or in writing to the Customer Advocacy Office. It is acknowledged
within 3 business days and given a case reference beginning CMP-.

A substantive response follows within 15 business days. Where the matter is
complex the customer is told before that deadline why more time is needed and
when a response can be expected; the outer limit is 35 business days from the
date of the complaint.

## If the customer is not satisfied with the outcome

A complaint response includes a final response notice. The customer may ask for
the case to be reviewed by a senior officer not involved in the original
decision, which adds up to 10 business days.

After a final response, or after 35 business days with no response, the customer
may refer the matter to the relevant external dispute resolution scheme. The
referral is free to the customer, and the scheme's decision binds the bank if
the customer accepts it. Time limits apply to external referral, generally six
months from the date of the final response.

## Electronic transfer errors - Regulation E

An error on an electronic funds transfer must be reported within 60 days of the
statement on which it first appeared. Reports may be made by phone, but the bank
may require written confirmation within 10 business days.

The investigation is completed within 10 business days. Where more time is
needed the investigation may take up to 45 days - extended to 90 days for
transfers that were initiated outside the country, that involve a point-of-sale
debit card transaction, or that occurred within 30 days of the first deposit to
a new account.

Where the investigation is extended, provisional credit for the disputed amount
is applied within 10 business days and the customer has full use of those funds
while the investigation continues. If the investigation finds no error, the
provisional credit is reversed after the customer is notified, with at least 5
business days' notice before the debit posts.

## Liability for unauthorised electronic transfers

Liability depends on how quickly the loss is reported, not on how the card or
credentials were compromised.

| Reported | Maximum customer liability |
| --- | --- |
| Before any unauthorised transfer occurs | $0 |
| Within 2 business days of learning of the loss | $50 |
| After 2 business days but within 60 days of the statement | $500 |
| More than 60 days after the statement | Unlimited for transfers after that point |

The clock runs from when the customer learns of the loss or theft, not from when
the transfer occurred. Extenuating circumstances - hospital admission, extended
travel - extend these periods on request.

## Billing errors on credit accounts - Regulation Z

A billing error on a credit card must be disputed in writing within 60 days of
the statement on which it appeared. The bank acknowledges within 30 days and
resolves within two billing cycles, and in no case more than 90 days.

The disputed amount and any related finance charges need not be paid while the
dispute is open, and the account may not be reported delinquent to a credit
bureau for that amount. Undisputed portions of the balance remain payable.

## Privacy and data rights

Customers may request a copy of the personal data held about them, ask for
inaccurate data to be corrected, and object to their data being used for
marketing. Requests are answered within 30 calendar days at no charge, extended
once by a further 60 days for complex requests.

Account and transaction records are retained for seven years after an account
closes, which is a regulatory requirement rather than a choice - a deletion
request cannot override it. Marketing preferences take effect within 10 business
days.

## Escalation matrix for staff

| Case type | First line | Escalate to | Escalate when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fee dispute under $100 | Contact centre | Team leader | Customer declines the first resolution |
| Fee dispute $100 and over | Team leader | Customer Advocacy | On receipt |
| Suspected fraud | Fraud operations | Financial Crime | Immediately, before contacting the customer |
| Regulation E dispute | Disputes team | Compliance | Day 40 if unresolved |
| Vulnerable customer indicator | Any agent | Vulnerable Customer Unit | On identification, same day |
| Media or regulator contact | Any agent | Corporate Affairs | Immediately, no substantive reply |
