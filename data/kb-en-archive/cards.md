# Cards - Debit and Credit

**doc_id:** KB-CARD-001
**owner:** Cards Operations
**last_reviewed:** 2026-05-14

## Reporting a lost or stolen card

A lost or stolen card can be blocked 24/7 through the mobile app (Cards → Manage →
Block), through this assistant, or by calling the 24-hour hotline on 1-800-555-0142.
A block takes effect immediately and cannot be reversed - a blocked card must be
replaced. Customers are not liable for unauthorised transactions reported within
60 days of the statement date on which they appear.

## Replacement cards

A replacement card is issued automatically when a card is blocked as lost or
stolen. Standard delivery is 5–7 business days and carries no fee for the first
replacement in a rolling 12-month period; subsequent replacements are $10 each.
Express delivery is 1–2 business days for a $25 fee. The card number changes, so
any recurring payments set up on the old card must be updated by the customer.

## Card limits

Default daily limits are $1,000 for ATM withdrawals and $5,000 for point-of-sale
purchases. Limits can be adjusted in the mobile app, or temporarily raised by an
agent for up to 72 hours. Limit changes above $10,000 per day require branch
verification with photo identification.

## Foreign transaction fees

Transactions in a currency other than USD carry a 2.5% foreign transaction fee on
standard debit and credit cards. The Premier Travel Card has no foreign
transaction fee. ATM withdrawals abroad additionally carry a flat $5 network fee.

## Contactless payments

Contactless is enabled by default on all cards issued after January 2024, with a
per-transaction cap of $100 before a PIN is requested. The cap is set by the card
network and cannot be changed at the customer's request.
