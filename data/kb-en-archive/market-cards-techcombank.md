## Techcombank Priority Visa Signature

The top of the range, restricted to customers already in the Priority or
Private segment.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | Waived |
| Credit limit | Up to VND 1 billion |
| Cashback | 10% on dining, hotels and travel |
| Travel insurance | Worldwide, cover up to VND 10.5 billion |
| Eligibility | Priority/Private membership, income from about VND 40 million/month |
| Security | Contactless payment, 3D Secure |

Two qualifications belong with that table whenever it is quoted.

The 10% cashback is a **category rate, not a blanket one**. It applies to the
listed merchant categories and, as with every cashback programme in this market,
is subject to a cap per statement cycle. A customer asking "so I get 10% back on
everything?" should be told plainly that they do not, and pointed at the issuer's
own programme terms for the cap.

The VND 40 million monthly income figure is a **segment threshold, not a card
application criterion**. A customer can meet the income level and still not hold
Priority status, in which case this card is not available to them. Presenting the
income figure as the qualifying condition sets up a rejection the customer was
told to expect approval on.

## "Lifetime free annual fee" - read the condition

Techcombank markets a group of cards as carrying no annual fee for life. Across
the Vietnamese card market that phrase almost always carries a maintenance
condition: a minimum number of transactions, or a spending total across the year.

When comparing, do not compress this to "that card is free". The condition is
precisely where customers complain after the first year, when a fee they were
told did not exist appears on a statement because a spending threshold was
missed.

## Where this document stops

Use it for directional comparison only. If a customer asks about:

- the balance, remaining limit or statement on **their own Techcombank card**
- the outcome of an application they have made to Techcombank
- a disputed transaction on a card Techcombank issued

that is the issuing bank's business, not this assistant's. Direct them to
Techcombank rather than speculating. This assistant cannot see another bank's
customer data and must never answer as though it can.
