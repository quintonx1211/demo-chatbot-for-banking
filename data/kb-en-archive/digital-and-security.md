# Online Banking, Disputes and Security

**doc_id:** KB-SEC-001
**owner:** Digital Channels / Fraud
**last_reviewed:** 2026-05-20

## Resetting an online banking password

A password can be reset from the login screen using "Forgot password", which sends
a one-time code to the registered mobile number or email. Three failed login
attempts lock the profile for 30 minutes; five failed attempts lock it until an
agent verifies the customer's identity. Passwords must be at least 12 characters
and cannot repeat any of the last five used.

## Registering a new device

A login from an unrecognised device requires a one-time passcode. Customers should
never share that passcode. Bank staff will never ask for a passcode, a password, or
a full card number, whether by phone, email, or chat.

## Disputing a transaction

A transaction can be disputed in online banking (Accounts → Transaction → Dispute)
or through an agent. Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the statement date
on which the transaction appeared. Provisional credit is applied within 10 business
days for debit-card disputes while the investigation runs; a full investigation
takes up to 45 days, extended to 90 days for transactions made abroad. Merchant
disputes should be raised with the merchant first - the bank requires evidence of
that attempt for non-fraud disputes.

## Recognising phishing

Legitimate bank messages never include a link to a login page and never threaten
immediate account closure. Suspected phishing should be forwarded to
security@example-bank.test and then deleted. If a customer has already entered
credentials on a suspicious page, the profile should be locked and the password
reset immediately.

## Mobile app

The mobile app supports biometric login, card freezing, mobile check deposit
(limit $5,000 per day), and Zelle transfers. Mobile deposits post the next business
day; funds above $500 may be held for two business days.
