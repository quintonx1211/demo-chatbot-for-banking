## MB Hi Collection - JCB and Visa

The distinguishing feature is that these are **combined cards**: credit card and
ATM card functions on a single piece of plastic, rather than two cards for the
same customer.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Card networks | JCB and Visa variants; a metal Visa variant exists |
| Combined function | Credit and ATM on one card |
| Card face | No printed name or card number on the Hi JCB Collection |
| Annual fee | Currently waived on several lines - see the conflict below |
| Contact | MB hotline 1900 545426 for the current schedule |

### The blank card face is a real security property

The Hi JCB Collection carries no printed name or card number. This removes the
most common physical compromise: a card photographed, or simply read, while it
sits on a table or is handed to a member of staff. Card details live in the app
instead.

It also has a practical consequence worth telling a customer before they choose
it. Any process that expects a human to read digits off the card - some phone
orders, some hotel guarantees, some older merchant terminals - requires opening
the app first. For a customer who travels to places where those processes are
still common, that is a genuine trade-off rather than a pure gain.

## The annual fee conflict, stated as a conflict

- MB's own material indicates annual fees are **currently waived** on Visa, JCB
  and Mastercard lines, with the policy subject to change.
- At least one independent review source reports a standing annual fee of **VND
  199,000 per year** for the regular JCB card.

These are not reconcilable from public sources, and the likeliest explanation is
that a promotional waiver is running over a standing fee. Do not quote either
figure as final. Tell the customer the fee is currently promoted as waived, that
the standing fee appears to be around VND 199,000, and that MB should be asked to
confirm which applies to the specific product and date.
