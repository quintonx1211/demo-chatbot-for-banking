## VPBank StepUp Mastercard

Positioned as an online-spending cashback card.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | About VND 499,000 per year |
| First-year waiver | Waived on 3 transactions of at least VND 300,000 each within the first 30 days |
| Renewal waiver | Waived where annual spending reaches VND 100 million |
| Online cashback | Up to 15%, capped at VND 600,000 per statement cycle |
| Dining cashback | Up to 4%, capped at VND 200,000 per statement cycle |
| Tier condition | The headline rates apply from VND 10 million spend per statement cycle |

### The exclusions are the important part

The cashback programme **excludes** Shopee transactions, bill payments and
insurance premiums. That exclusion list deserves to be stated before the headline
rate rather than after it, because the card is widely understood in the market as
an e-commerce cashback card and Shopee is the transaction a customer is most
likely to assume is covered.

The tiering is equally easy to misread. The 15% rate is not the rate for all
online spending - it applies once total spending in the statement cycle reaches
VND 10 million, and the VND 600,000 cap means the effective ceiling is reached at
VND 4 million of qualifying online spend. Beyond that point the marginal rate is
zero.

## VPBank Lady

Aimed at a category mix rather than a channel.

| Category | Cashback |
|---|---|
| Online insurance and education | Up to 15% |
| Beauty and medical | Up to 10% |
| Supermarkets | Up to 5% |
| Annual cap, all categories | VND 12,000,000 per year |

The annual cap of VND 12 million across all categories is the number that
determines what this card is actually worth. It is a generous ceiling by market
standards, but the per-category caps and qualifying conditions sit inside it, so
the headline rates and the annual cap cannot simply be multiplied together.

## Comparing a waived fee against a conditional waiver

Both VPBank waivers above are **conditional**, and the conditions differ between
the first year and renewal. A customer who clears the first-year condition - three
transactions in thirty days - has done nothing towards the renewal condition,
which requires VND 100 million of annual spending. A customer told "the annual fee
is waived" in month one will reasonably believe it stays waived, which is the
complaint that arrives thirteen months later.
