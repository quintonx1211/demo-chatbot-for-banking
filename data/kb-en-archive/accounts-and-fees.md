# Accounts, Fees and Transfers

**doc_id:** KB-ACCT-001
**owner:** Retail Banking
**last_reviewed:** 2026-05-02

## Opening an account

A checking or savings account can be opened online in about 10 minutes, or at any
branch. Applicants need a government-issued photo ID, a Social Security number or
ITIN, proof of address dated within 90 days, and an opening deposit of $25 for
checking or $100 for savings. Accounts for applicants under 18 require a joint
adult account holder.

## Monthly maintenance fees

Everyday Checking carries a $12 monthly fee, waived with a $1,500 minimum daily
balance or $500 in monthly direct deposits. Student Checking has no monthly fee
for account holders aged 17–24. Premier Checking carries a $25 monthly fee, waived
with $25,000 in combined deposit balances.

## Overdraft

Overdraft coverage is opt-in. The overdraft fee is $34 per item, capped at three
items per day. No fee is charged when an account is overdrawn by $5 or less, or
when the balance is restored by the end of the next business day. Overdraft
protection linking a savings account transfers funds automatically for a $10
transfer fee.

## Transfers

Internal transfers between accounts at this bank are instant and free. ACH
transfers to other US banks take 1–3 business days and are free for standard
delivery; same-day ACH is $5. Domestic wires cost $25 outgoing and $15 incoming.
International wires cost $45 outgoing and settle in 1–5 business days depending on
the destination country. The daily cut-off for same-day processing is 4:00 PM ET.

## Statements

Electronic statements are free and available for the last 7 years in online
banking. Paper statements cost $3 per month. A certified copy of a historical
statement requested through a branch costs $10 per statement.

## Branch and support hours

Branches are open Monday to Friday 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM,
closed Sundays and federal holidays. The phone support centre is staffed Monday to
Friday 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM ET and Saturday to Sunday 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM ET. Card
blocking and fraud reporting are available 24/7.
