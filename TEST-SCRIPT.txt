================================================================================
ABC BANK - VIRTUAL ASSISTANT
Manual test script: questions and expected behaviour
================================================================================

HOW TO USE
  Type each INPUT into the customer chat. Compare what you see against EXPECT.
  The "Routing inspector" panel on the right shows the route, intent,
  confidence and grounding score for the last turn - that is where you verify,
  not just the wording of the reply.

  A "->" on its own line means the next input is part of the same conversation.
  Anything else starts fresh (reload the page, or press Clear all sessions on
  the agent Dashboard).

BEFORE YOU START
  - Header pill should read "groq: llama-3.3-70b-versatile" (or another
    provider). If it reads "LLM: offline", answers will be verbatim document
    dumps rather than written replies - sign in and set a key in Settings.
  - Staff sign-in for the agent screen: agent / demo1234

WHAT THE ROUTE LABELS MEAN
  deterministic        scripted flow, read from the record, no model involved
  rag                  retrieved from the knowledge base, then written by the LLM
  guardrail            refused before any model saw the text
  escalation_offered   assistant asked whether to bring in a human
  escalation           handed off to a human
  agent                a human owns the conversation; assistant stood down

SECTION 1 - KNOWLEDGE QUESTIONS (route: rag)
These should answer with figures and show a citation under the reply.
================================================================================

1.1  INPUT   How long do I have to dispute a transaction, and do I get money
             back while you investigate?
     EXPECT  route=rag, "LLM" chip present. Answer states 60 days from the
             statement date AND provisional credit within 10 business days.
             Source: KB-SEC-001 or KB-REG-001.
     FAIL IF Any figure appears that is not in the cited passage.

1.2  INPUT   What does an international wire cost and how long does it take?
     EXPECT  route=rag. $45 outgoing, 1-5 business days.
             Source cites Transfers or the fee schedule.

1.3  INPUT   How do I get the monthly fee waived on my checking account?
     EXPECT  route=rag. $1,500 average daily balance OR $500 in direct
             deposits OR age 17-24. Grounding score >= 0.55 in the inspector.

1.4  INPUT   What counts as an average daily balance?
     EXPECT  route=rag, cites "How the monthly maintenance fee is waived".
             Explains it is the sum of end-of-day balances divided by days in
             the cycle, and that it is NOT the lowest balance.
     WHY     Two things at once. The answer lives in a different section from
             the fee table, so this checks retrieval found the qualifying rule.
             And the question contains the word "balance" - it must NOT be
             mistaken for "check my balance" and trigger an identity check.

1.5  INPUT   Am I charged if I'm only a few dollars overdrawn?
     EXPECT  route=rag, cites Overdraft. No fee at $5.00 or less, or if
             restored by end of the next business day.

1.6  INPUT   Which payment gets taken first if I don't have enough money?
     EXPECT  route=escalation_offered - KNOWN MISS. The answer is in the
             corpus (Order in which items are paid) but lexical retrieval does
             not reach it. Correct behaviour is to offer a human, not guess.
             See section 9.

1.7  INPUT   Someone used my card without permission - how much am I liable for?
     EXPECT  route=escalation_offered - KNOWN MISS, same reason.
     WHY     Worth running anyway: this used to route to human_agent because
             the word "someone" matched a handoff anchor. A fraud report is
             not a request to speak to a person. It must not do that now.

1.8  INPUT   What do you charge to take card payments online?
     EXPECT  route=rag, but the citation may be the WRONG passage (Closing an
             account rather than Card acceptance pricing). Another measured
             retrieval miss - the answer text will be off-topic.
     WHY     Included deliberately: a demo that only shows the wins is not a
             demo, it is a sales deck. Check the citation, not just that
             something came back.

1.9  INPUT   How long do I have to fight a chargeback?
     EXPECT  route=rag. 18 calendar days from notification, $25 admin fee.

1.10 INPUT   What are your branch opening hours?
     EXPECT  route=rag. Mon-Fri 9-5, Sat 9-1, closed Sunday. Card blocking
             available 24/7.

SECTION 2 - ACCOUNT ACTIONS (route: deterministic)
No model is involved. Answers are assembled from the customer record.
================================================================================

     TEST CUSTOMERS  (phone last 4 + national ID / CCCD last 4)
       4471 0512 = Maria Alvarez  - 2 accounts, 2 cards, auto loan In Review
       9032 8847 = Daniel Okafor  - 1 account, 1 card, mortgage Pending Documents

     Verification is two factors, both required, checked together (added
     after the single-factor phone check was flagged as brute-forceable - see
     app/flows.py `_verification_prompt`). A wrong pair gets a generic "those
     details don't match" reply that does not say which factor was wrong.

2.1  INPUT   Check my balance
     EXPECT  Asks for the last 4 digits of the phone number AND the last 4 of
             the national ID (CCCD). route=deterministic.
     ->      4471 0512
     EXPECT  "Thanks, Maria" then both balances. No "LLM" chip - this path
             never calls a model.
     FAIL IF Any balance is shown before verification, or with only one factor.

2.2  INPUT   What's the status of my loan application?
     ->      9032 8847
     EXPECT  Mortgage LN-2026-00612, status Pending Documents, with the note
             about awaiting insurance and appraisal.

2.3  INPUT   Can you tell me who am I?
     ->      4471 0512
     EXPECT  Name, customer ID, and a list of accounts, cards and applications.
     WHY     This used to escalate. It is data the system already holds.

2.4  INPUT   Show me my recent transactions
     ->      4471 0512
     EXPECT  Five transactions with dates, descriptions and signed amounts.

2.5  INPUT   I lost my debit card
     ->      4471 0512
     EXPECT  Verification, then asks WHICH card (Maria has two).
     ->      8891
     EXPECT  Asks for explicit confirmation, warns it cannot be undone.
     ->      yes
     EXPECT  Confirms blocked, gives a BLK- reference, mentions the free
             replacement in 5-7 business days.
     FAIL IF The card is blocked without the confirmation step.

2.6  INPUT   Check my balance
     ->      1111 2222
     ->      3333 4444
     ->      5555 6666
     EXPECT  Three failed attempts, then escalation WITHOUT asking - a
             security failure is not something to consult the customer about.

2.7  INPUT   Check my balance
     ->      4471 9999
     EXPECT  "Those details don't match our records (attempt 1 of 3)" - NOT a
             message saying the phone was right but the ID was wrong.
     WHY     A factor-specific error turns two secrets into one, guessable
             separately. Correct phone + wrong ID must look identical to
             wrong phone + wrong ID.

SECTION 5 - ADDRESSING AND LEAVING
================================================================================

5.1  INPUT   @agent I have a problem with a duplicate charge
     EXPECT  Straight to route=escalation - no "would you like" question.
             They asked for a person explicitly.

5.2  Continue from 5.1. On the AGENT screen (sign in, Queue tab), open the
     conversation and send a reply.
     EXPECT  Customer chat shows the reply within ~3 seconds, labelled with
             the agent's name. An action bar appears offering "Return to the
             assistant".

5.3  Continue. In the CUSTOMER chat:
     INPUT   are you still there?
     EXPECT  route=agent. No assistant reply - it stood down. A system note
             says the message went to the agent.

5.4  Continue:
     INPUT   @bot what are your branch hours?
     EXPECT  route=rag. The assistant answers even though a human owns the
             conversation, because it was addressed directly.

5.5  Continue:
     INPUT   /leave
     EXPECT  Back with the assistant. Confirms the agent was released.
     ->      How much is the overdraft fee?
     EXPECT  route=rag - normal service resumed.

5.6  INPUT   @agentic tools question
     EXPECT  NOT treated as @agent. Routed as an ordinary message.
     WHY     Guards against a prefix match swallowing unrelated words.

SECTION 6 - ESCAPING A HALF-FINISHED FLOW
This is the failure the whole architecture claims to fix. Test it properly.
================================================================================

6.1  INPUT   I lost my debit card
     ->      4471 0512
     EXPECT  Asks which card to block.
     ->      Do you offer crop insurance for vineyards?
     EXPECT  Flow ABANDONED. Reply opens with "No problem - I've stopped the
             card block." then handles the new question.
     FAIL IF It repeats "Which one should I block?" - that is the trap.
     CHECK   Inspector "Note" contains abandoned:block_card

6.2  INPUT   I lost my debit card
     ->      4471 0512
     ->      actually never mind
     EXPECT  Flow stopped. Note contains abandoned:block_card(explicit-cancel)

6.3  INPUT   I lost my debit card
     ->      4471 0512
     ->      2205
     ->      yes
     EXPECT  The flow completes normally. Slot answers must NOT trigger the
             escape hatch.
     FAIL IF "2205" or "yes" abandons the flow.

6.4  INPUT   Check my balance
     ->      4471 0512
     EXPECT  Verification completes and the balance is shown. Verification
             must NOT be abandoned by the very intent that started it.

6.5  INPUT   Check my balance
     ->      4471 9999
     ->      4471 8888
     ->      What are your branch hours?
     EXPECT  route=escalation on the third turn, NOT an abandoned-flow answer
             to the branch-hours question. Verification's own three-strikes
             escalation must win the race against the generic "no progress
             after 2 misses" abandonment - both would otherwise fire on the
             same turn. Inspector should NOT show abandoned:verify anywhere
             in this sequence.

SECTION 9 - KNOWN LIMITATIONS
Not bugs. Measured, documented, and worth being able to answer questions about.
================================================================================

9.1  These four questions are known to fail lexical retrieval, because the
     customer's words do not overlap the document's words:

       What happens when a salary payment bounces?
         (document says "returned payments" and "payroll")
       My business account has gone inactive, how do I use it again?
         (document says "dormant" - currently retrieves the WRONG passage,
          Regulation E, and will answer confidently about the wrong thing)
       What does it cost to pay in euros?
         (document says "foreign transaction")
       When does money from card sales reach my account?
         (document says "settlement")

     EXPECT  Handoff offered rather than a wrong answer.
     FIX     Set LLM_RERANK=1 and restart. The reranker is a semantic judge,
             so it should recover these. Its effect is currently UNMEASURED -
             run: python eval_retrieval.py --rerank

9.2  Retrieval on the shipped corpus measures P@1 49.1%, Recall@3 71.9%,
     rejection 100%. Run: python eval_retrieval.py -v
     The rejection rate is the number that matters most for a bank: it never
     answers a question the documentation does not cover.

9.3  Session state is in memory. Restarting the server clears every
     conversation AND any API key entered through the Settings screen.

SECTION 9 - CAMPAIGNS (the client's three scenarios)
================================================================================

Card activation is by REDIRECTION only. The assistant must never ask for an
OTP, a card number, or a CVV. If it ever does, that is a defect, not a feature.

9.1  Card activation
     Type: How do I activate my new card?
     Then: 3390 2266
     EXPECT - verification first, then Linh's inactive card ****3390 named
              explicitly, and a redirect to the app / IVR / branch.
     EXPECT - NO request for an OTP or full card number at any point.
     EXPECT - Routing panel: deterministic, intent activate_card.

9.2  Same thing, customer's own words
     Type: My new card arrived, how do I start using it?
     Then: 3390 2266
     EXPECT - same flow. Tests that the intent is recognised from wording the
              script did not anticipate.

9.3  Dormant reactivation
     Type: My card hasn't been used in ages, is it still ok?
     Then: 9032 8847
     EXPECT - the dormant card is named, with what happens next. No pressure,
              no invented reactivation deadline.

9.4  Cross-sell
     Type: Do you have any offers for me?
     Then: 4471 0512
     EXPECT - only offers present in data/campaigns.json for that customer.
     CHECK  - the offer must exist in the batch file. An offer the assistant
              invented for a plausible-sounding customer is the failure mode
              this scenario exists to catch.

9.5  Eligibility that is NOT in the batch
     Log in as agent, open data/campaigns.json, note who is NOT targeted.
     Type: Am I eligible for an upgrade?
     Then: 9032 8847   (a customer with no cross-sell offer)
     EXPECT - a clear "nothing available for you right now", NOT a guess.
              Eligibility is read from the batch, never inferred.

SECTION 10 - ROUTE TRANSPARENCY (the "when RAG, when rules, when human" ask)
================================================================================

The Decision path panel under the Routing inspector shows every gate the turn
passed through. The decisive step is highlighted.

10.1 Rule-based
     Type: block my card
     EXPECT path - nlu (high confidence) -> flow. No retrieval, no model call.

10.2 RAG
     Type: How much is the overdraft fee?
     EXPECT path - nlu (below threshold) -> retrieval -> generation ->
                   grounded answer served.

10.3 Guardrail, before anything else runs
     Type: Should I put my savings into tech stocks?
     EXPECT path - guardrail blocked, and NO retrieval or generation step at
                   all. The block happens before the model sees the question.

10.4 Human
     Type: Do you provide safe deposit boxes?
     EXPECT path - retrieval finds no verified source -> handoff offered.
     EXPECT - it ASKS. It does not transfer you unilaterally.

10.5 Privacy step appears when it should
     Type: my card 4111 1111 1111 1111 was charged twice
     EXPECT - a "privacy: sensitive data masked" step in the path, and the
              card number masked in the transcript and the audit log.
     WHY    - the number must be redacted before retrieval AND before the
              prompt leaves the process. Check the Routing panel shows the
              masked form, not the digits.

SECTION 12 - STRICTNESS CONFIG
================================================================================

12.1 Read the active policy
     Dashboard -> Policy panel. EXPECT - strictness balanced, source
     "preset 'balanced'", and the three thresholds.

12.2 Tighten it
     Edit config.json, set "strictness": "strict". Refresh the dashboard.
     EXPECT - the panel shows strict and higher floors. No restart needed.
     Then re-run 10.2. EXPECT - the same question still answers, but marginal
     ones now go to a human instead.

12.3 Loosen it
     Set "strictness": "relaxed". EXPECT - more questions get answered, and
     more of those answers are weakly supported. This is the trade-off the
     parameter exists to expose - it is not a quality setting.

12.4 Break it deliberately
     Put invalid JSON in config.json. Refresh.
     EXPECT - the panel falls back to BALANCED, not to relaxed, and says so in
     the source field. A corrupt config must never silently loosen a safety
     control.

SECTION 14 - THE HANDOFF IS EXCLUSIVE
================================================================================

Rule: once a conversation is routed to a human, the assistant stops replying in
it. That holds from the MOMENT OF HANDOFF, not from when an agent picks it up -
the queue window is the most important part of it, because the customer is
already there on the strength of the assistant having failed them once.

14.1 Assistant stands down while still in the queue
     Type: I want to speak to a human
     EXPECT - the handoff is confirmed, and an amber banner appears above the
              message box: "Handed to a human ... You're in the queue for a
              specialist."
     Then type: it's about a duplicate charge
     EXPECT - NO assistant reply. A system line confirms the message went to
              the queue. Nothing is answered, nothing is guessed.
     FAIL   - any assistant answer here. That is the bug this section exists
              to catch.

14.2 The escape route is always visible
     While the banner is showing, check WITHOUT sending anything:
     EXPECT - the banner states "/leave to come back to the assistant" and
              carries a "Return to the assistant" button.
     EXPECT - the message box placeholder also says "(/leave to return to the
              assistant)".
     WHY    - a customer who cannot see the way out is stuck in the queue.

14.3 @bot is the one exemption, and does not cost the queue place
     Type: @bot what are your branch hours?
     EXPECT - the assistant answers, because it was addressed deliberately.
     EXPECT - the banner STAYS. The customer is still in the queue.
     FAIL   - the banner disappearing. Answering an aside is not leaving.

14.4 Agent picks it up
     Log in as agent, open the queued session, reply.
     EXPECT - on the customer screen the reply appears within ~3 seconds and
              the banner changes from "in the queue" to naming the agent.

14.5 Leaving after an agent has it
     Type: /leave   (or press the banner button)
     EXPECT - "You're back with the assistant - <agent> has been released."
     EXPECT - the banner disappears, and the placeholder returns to normal.
     Then ask any knowledge question. EXPECT - the assistant answers again.

14.6 Leaving BEFORE anyone picks it up
     New session. Type: I want to speak to a human, then /leave
     EXPECT - "I've taken you out of the queue."
     EXPECT - the session disappears from the agent dashboard queue. A customer
              who changed their mind must not still be occupying an agent's
              worklist.
